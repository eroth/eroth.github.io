So it’s been a really, really, really, really, really long time since I’ve written anything for this blog! Some things have been happening behind the scenes (moved the blog to use the Minimal Mistakes theme as a dependency instead of just using it as a fork) but not much content.

Looking forward to sharing loads of cool tech stuff and projects I’ve been working on! As always, any questions just drop a line!
